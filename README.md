# 🚀 Overview

**SnapNEarn** is an AI-powered traffic violation reporting platform designed to improve road safety by connecting citizens, intelligent computer vision systems, and traffic authorities.

The platform allows users to submit photographic or video evidence of traffic violations. AI-assisted computer vision can analyze the submitted evidence for relevant violations, while the backend securely manages authentication, reports, evidence, locations, and status updates.

The goal is to create a scalable digital ecosystem that makes traffic violation reporting **faster, smarter, more transparent, and accessible**.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Traffic violations are often difficult to monitor because authorities cannot be physically present everywhere at all times.

Common challenges include:

- Limited traffic surveillance coverage
- Delayed reporting of violations
- Manual verification processes
- Difficulty managing large amounts of evidence
- Privacy concerns around uploaded media
- Lack of transparent report tracking

SnapNEarn addresses these challenges by combining:

**Citizen Reporting + AI Computer Vision + Secure Backend + Real-Time Communication**
